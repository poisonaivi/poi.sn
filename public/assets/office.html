logos

Recommendations:

  • When scaling, pixel art should be scaled using nearest-neighbor interpolation.
  • When using just the brandmark, 'favicon-l' is preferable for most cases.
  • When using the brand/wordmark combination, logo-horizontal is preferable for most cases.
  • Recolor these assets as needed, but if so, maintain a single, solid color.
(change filename ending from -l to -m and -s for medium and small variants)
